01 SCORE THE GROUPS
Every score box is a button. Tap once for 0, and each tap adds a goal — so a 2–1 needs three taps on one side, two on the other. Tap past 9 to clear a box. Hover a scored match and a small × appears at the row's right edge to reset just that match.
Standings update live with official tiebreakers. When all six matches are set, the group locks ✓ and the top two highlight green.
02 THE BEST THIRDS
New for 48 teams: the eight best third-placed sides also advance. Once all 12 groups are complete, the strip below the groups ranks all twelve 3rd-place teams — green chips are in, faded chips are out. A qualifying third also gets an amber row in its group table.
Ranking criteria: points → goal difference → goals scored.
03 PICK THE BRACKET
The knockout tab shows the official match map — Round of 32 through the final at MetLife. Slots fill themselves as groups complete. Tap a team to advance it; tap again to unpick. Change a group result and any picks it breaks clear automatically.
The whole bracket scales to fit your window — no sideways scrolling.
04 AUTO-FILL
Short on time? ⚡ Auto-fill remaining simulates everything you haven't predicted — goals follow a ranking-weighted model, favorites advance 82% of the time, upsets happen 18%. Your own entries are never overwritten.
Run it after picking your favorites by hand — it only fills the blanks.
05 ENTER REAL RESULTS
The EDITING toggle (right of the tabs) switches to Actual results — a separate dataset for what really happens, shown with gold borders. Enter real scores as matches finish; your prediction stays untouched in its own mode.
CLEAR only wipes the mode you're currently editing.
06 THE SCORECARD
The third tab grades your bracket against reality: 3 pts for an exact group score, 1 pt for the right outcome, and escalating points for every team you correctly put through — +1 R32 · +2 R16 · +4 QF · +8 SF · +16 Final · +32 Champion.
Knockout credit is path-independent — if your team gets there, you score.
07 PATH TO GLORY
Click any team's row in a standings table to see their full predicted story — three group games with W/D/L badges, where they finish, and every knockout round until they fall… or lift the trophy.
Works in both modes — in Actual results it shows their real road so far.
08 SHARE IT
Finish every prediction and two buttons appear: SHARE sends a text summary (champion, final, semifinalists — with flag emoji) via your share sheet or clipboard; CARD downloads a 1200×630 image of your bracket, made for the group chat.
Everything saves automatically in your browser — close the tab, come back, it's all there.
